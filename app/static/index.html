A near-real-time and periodic data warehouse for a retail chain

Online sales stream in one by one, store sales arrive once an hour. MESHJOIN joins both with the customer and product Master Data on disk, sheds store sales when the stream is too fast, and loads every sale into a MySQL star schema. Click a station to open that part.
